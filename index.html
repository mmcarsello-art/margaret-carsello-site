Practice
The vessel as a recurring form — something that holds, contains, and sometimes fails to.

Working across clay, video, and the body, the work circles a few persistent questions: what a container reveals about what it's built to hide, how material carries memory, and how the self gets performed — on a pedestal, on a screen, or in front of a camera.
